# Draft interview guide (not approved for fieldwork)

Use this only after the appropriate institutional and ethical permission has been obtained.

## Questions for authorized blood-bank staff
1. How are volunteers registered, contacted and referred to approved donation centres?
2. How are donation campaigns proposed, authorized and communicated to organizers?
3. Which capabilities are already available in the NBTS blood-bank or appointment systems?
4. What work remains manual, if any, and how is the amount of work measured?
5. Which reports would be useful and which could pose confidentiality or patient-safety risks?
6. Can volunteer organizers coordinate campaigns without access to patient records?
7. Which roles can legitimately see information across multiple hospitals?
8. What would constitute a useful but safe, limited research evaluation?

## Questions for approved volunteer-club participants
1. How are donation events planned and attended?
2. How do volunteers express or revoke permission to receive notifications?
3. Which languages and communication channels do participants prefer?
4. What concerns exist around visibility of blood group and contact details?
5. How can organizers avoid inappropriate pressure or unsolicited contact?

Do not request personal medical histories or named patient cases in an initial requirements interview. Do not infer that hospital participation or NBTS approval exists.
